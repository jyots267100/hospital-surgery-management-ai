TITLE: Operating Room Policy
DOCUMENT ID: POL-OR-004
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-01-01
OWNER: Department of Surgical Operations

--- PAGE 1 ---

SECTION 1: PURPOSE

This policy governs the allocation and use of operating rooms (ORs)
across the surgical department.

SECTION 2: ROOM DESIGNATIONS

2.1 Each operating room has a designated primary use. OR-5 is designated
    as the Emergency operating room and is prioritized for emergency
    cases at any time of day.
2.2 OR-1 through OR-4 are general-purpose operating rooms available for
    routine and urgent procedures during standard scheduling hours.

--- PAGE 2 ---

SECTION 3: AVAILABILITY CHECKS

3.1 Before a surgery is scheduled or rescheduled into a room, the system
    must verify that the room has no overlapping booking for the
    requested date and time window.
3.2 Availability queries (for example "which rooms are available
    tomorrow between 2 PM and 4 PM") must check all active, non-cancelled
    surgeries in that room for the requested window and report any
    conflicting surgery.

SECTION 4: MAINTENANCE AND BLACKOUT WINDOWS

4.1 Operating rooms may be temporarily unavailable for scheduled
    maintenance. This portfolio system does not model maintenance
    blackout windows; in a production deployment, maintenance windows
    would be stored alongside the room record and checked during
    availability queries.

--- PAGE 3 ---

SECTION 5: UTILIZATION REPORTING

5.1 Operating room utilization should be reported as the percentage of
    available operating time (assumed to be an 8-hour operating day per
    room) that is booked by active, non-cancelled surgeries.

END OF DOCUMENT.
