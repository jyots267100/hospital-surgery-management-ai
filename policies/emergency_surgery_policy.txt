SECTION 1: PURPOSE

This policy describes how emergency surgeries are prioritized and
scheduled outside of normal elective workflows.

SECTION 2: CLASSIFICATION

2.1 A surgery is classified as "emergency" priority when a delay in
    treatment would pose an immediate risk to the patient's life or
    long-term health.
2.2 Emergency surgeries may be scheduled into OR-5 (the designated
    emergency operating room) at any time, including outside standard
    operating hours, and may take precedence over routine or urgent
    cases when no emergency room is immediately free.

--- PAGE 2 ---

SECTION 3: SCHEDULING OVERRIDES

3.1 Emergency surgeries are still subject to operating-room and
    surgeon-conflict checks; the system does not allow double-booking
    even for emergencies. Staff must select an alternative available
    room or surgeon when a true conflict exists.
3.2 Emergency surgery creation still requires confirmation from the
    requesting clinician before the record is finalized, consistent with
    the Surgery Scheduling Policy.

SECTION 4: CANCELLATION OF EMERGENCY CASES

4.1 An emergency surgery may only be cancelled if the treating surgeon
    confirms the procedure is no longer clinically indicated, per the
    Surgery Cancellation Policy, Section 4.3.

END OF DOCUMENT.
