TITLE: Surgery Delay Policy
DOCUMENT ID: POL-DELAY-003
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-01-01
OWNER: Department of Surgical Operations

--- PAGE 1 ---

SECTION 1: PURPOSE

This policy defines how delays to scheduled surgeries are recorded and
communicated, so operations staff can monitor operating room throughput
and patient impact.

SECTION 2: WHEN A SURGERY IS CONSIDERED DELAYED

2.1 A surgery is considered delayed when its actual or expected start
    time is later than its originally scheduled time by more than 15
    minutes.
2.2 Delays must be recorded with both a delay duration in minutes and a
    delay reason, for example: "preceding case overran", "awaiting
    clearance", "equipment preparation", or "surgeon delayed in another
    procedure".

--- PAGE 2 ---

SECTION 3: RECORDING DELAYS

3.1 When a surgery is marked as delayed, the system must update its
    status to "delayed" and record delay_minutes and delay_reason.
3.2 A delayed surgery may subsequently transition to "confirmed",
    "in_progress", or "cancelled" once the delay is resolved or the
    decision is made to cancel.
3.3 Marking a surgery as delayed is a status transition and should be
    logged as an audit event noting the previous status, new status, and
    delay reason.

SECTION 4: ESCALATION

4.1 Delays exceeding 60 minutes should be flagged to the operations
    dashboard as high-impact delays so coordinators can notify affected
    patients and downstream procedures.
4.2 Repeated delays attributed to the same operating room or surgeon on
    the same day should be reviewed by the operations coordinator.

--- PAGE 3 ---

SECTION 5: REPORTING

5.1 The operational dashboard should expose the average delay in minutes
    across all delayed surgeries for the current reporting period.
5.2 Delay data must never be deleted; it is retained as part of the
    surgery's audit history.

END OF DOCUMENT.
