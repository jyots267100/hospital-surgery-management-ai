TITLE: Surgery Scheduling Policy
DOCUMENT ID: POL-SCHED-002
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-01-01
OWNER: Department of Surgical Operations

--- PAGE 1 ---

SECTION 1: PURPOSE

This policy governs how new surgeries are scheduled into operating rooms
and assigned to surgeons, to avoid conflicts and ensure patient safety.

SECTION 2: REQUIRED INFORMATION

A new surgery request must include:

2.1 A valid, registered patient identifier.
2.2 A surgery type/procedure description.
2.3 A valid, registered surgeon identifier.
2.4 A valid, registered operating room identifier.
2.5 A requested date and start time.
2.6 An estimated duration in minutes. If omitted, a default duration
    appropriate to the procedure type should be used.
2.7 A priority classification: routine, urgent, or emergency.

--- PAGE 2 ---

SECTION 3: CONFLICT PREVENTION

3.1 An operating room may not host two overlapping surgeries. The system
    must reject or flag any request that would double-book a room.
3.2 A surgeon may not be assigned to two overlapping surgeries. The
    system must reject or flag any request that would double-book a
    surgeon.
3.3 When a conflict is detected, the system should surface the
    conflicting surgery identifier so staff can choose an alternative
    time, room, or surgeon.

SECTION 4: PRIORITY HANDLING

4.1 Emergency surgeries may be scheduled into any operating room
    designated for emergency use, including outside of normal business
    hours.
4.2 Routine and urgent surgeries should preferentially be scheduled
    during standard operating hours (08:00-18:00) unless clinically
    necessary otherwise.

--- PAGE 3 ---

SECTION 5: CONFIRMATION REQUIREMENT

5.1 Creating a new surgery is a state-changing action. The proposed
    surgery details must be presented to the requesting staff member for
    confirmation before the record is created.
5.2 Every created surgery must generate an audit event recording the
    actor, timestamp, and the full set of scheduled details.

SECTION 6: EXAMPLES

Example A: "Schedule a knee replacement for patient P102 with Dr. Sharma
tomorrow at 10 AM in OR-2" is a valid routine scheduling request provided
Dr. Sharma and OR-2 are both free at that time.

Example B: A request that would place a new surgery in an operating room
already booked for another procedure at the same time must be rejected
with a clear conflict explanation.

END OF DOCUMENT.
