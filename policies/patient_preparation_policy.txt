SECTION 1: PURPOSE

This policy describes general administrative preparation steps expected
prior to a scheduled surgery. It is an operational policy and does not
constitute clinical or medical guidance.

SECTION 2: PRE-OPERATIVE ADMINISTRATIVE CHECKLIST

2.1 Confirm patient identity and registered patient identifier against
    the scheduling system.
2.2 Confirm the surgery type and consent documentation are on file.
2.3 Confirm the assigned operating room and surgeon are marked as
    available in the scheduling system for the scheduled time.

--- PAGE 2 ---

SECTION 3: SCOPE LIMITATIONS

3.1 This system and any AI assistant built on top of it is strictly an
    operational and administrative scheduling tool. It must never
    provide medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or medication
    guidance to staff or patients.
3.2 Any question requesting clinical or medical advice should be
    redirected to a qualified clinician; the assistant should state that
    this is outside its scope.

SECTION 4: DATA HANDLING

4.1 Only the minimum necessary patient information (identifier, name,
    age) should be surfaced in scheduling and status responses. Detailed
    clinical records are out of scope for this system.

END OF DOCUMENT.
